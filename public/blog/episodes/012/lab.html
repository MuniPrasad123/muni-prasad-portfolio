What the protections actually mean

Every worker uses token-safe unlock. That protects B's Redis key, not the stock row. The atomic database update prevents a negative inventory count. Fencing additionally rejects A's old generation after B's newer generation is accepted by the database.

Generations 41 and 42 are illustrative. The lab has no real token issuer, Redis, database, payment provider, or persistence. Replaying the animation does not change its counters.
