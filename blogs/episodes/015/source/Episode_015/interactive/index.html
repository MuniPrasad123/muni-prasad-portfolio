Architecture and failure handling

Signed webhook → raw-body signature check → PostgreSQL unique inbox → guarded payment transition + transactional outbox → async worker with downstream idempotency.

If ACK is lost after commit, the provider retries. Unique inbox prevents repeating the payment transition. External side effects still require their own idempotency.
