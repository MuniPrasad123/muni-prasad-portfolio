# Rebuild video
Requires ffmpeg and espeak. Generate English voiceover with `espeak -s 150 -w narration.wav -f voiceover.txt`. Render a 1080x1920 motion design with moving event packets, stage transitions, readable captions, and mux AAC narration. Verify with `ffprobe -show_streams`. The included MP4 is a lightweight generated motion explainer, not a studio-produced 3D animation.
