00–11s · CUSTOMER
Maya pays $120 once. Her bank confirms one transaction. Then three order-confirmation emails arrive.

11–22s · WHAT WENT WRONG?
The payment provider sent a signed success webhook. Our server processed it, but its acknowledgment was lost.

22–33s · RETRY
The provider retries the same event. An unsafe webhook handler sends another email and shipment request.

33–45s · INBOX
First fix: verify the signature and save the provider event ID in a durable PostgreSQL inbox. Duplicates stop here.

45–56s · STATE GUARD
Second: transition the payment from pending to succeeded only once, even if different event IDs describe the same payment.

56–69s · OUTBOX
Third: commit one outbox intent with the payment update. A separate worker delivers it with stable downstream idempotency keys.

69–82s · CUSTOMER PROTECTED
Now repeated webhooks receive a safe acknowledgment without repeating the business action. One payment, one logical confirmation, one fulfillment intent.
