# Production instructions
No MP4/audio was rendered because natural narration quality could not be verified. Use a licensed natural English voice with a human quality review; do not claim speech quality based solely on presence of an audio stream. Render 1080x1920, 30 fps, 68 seconds. Build one fixed 2×3 six-panel composition; highlight each panel in order with motion paths and moving request packets. Mix narration above a very low music bed. Burn `captions.srt` into the output video and include separate SRT/VTT. Inspect first, middle, and last subtitle frames and listen to the full audio. Confirm with ffprobe that both video and audio streams exist. Example subtitle overlay once an MP4 with narration exists: `ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf subtitles=captions.srt -c:v libx264 -c:a copy output_captioned.mp4`. Never treat this instruction as proof of rendering.
