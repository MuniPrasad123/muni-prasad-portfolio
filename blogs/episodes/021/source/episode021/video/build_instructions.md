# Video production specification
Target 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 72 seconds, H.264 MP4 with AAC narration. Six persistent clean 2×3 panels, readable type >=44px for 1080px width, safe margins 80px. Use storyboard and voiceover, produce original animated event packets and zoom emphasis. Natural English voice actor or approved high-quality neural voice; do not substitute robotic speech. Generate captions from actual spoken timing, burn them in using FFmpeg `subtitles=video/captions.srt` after synchronization; include external SRT/VTT. Listen to narration, inspect caption legibility, ffprobe for audio and video streams, and review sample frames. No video/audio rendered in this run.
