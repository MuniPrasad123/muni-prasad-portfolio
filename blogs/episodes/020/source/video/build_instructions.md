# Video production
1. Record or synthesize a licensed natural, expressive English voice reading `voiceover.txt`; listen to verify quality. 2. Animate the six-panel SVG as a persistent 1080×1920 grid; move request packets between panels. 3. Time to the 72-second storyboard. 4. Burn `captions.srt` into the video with FFmpeg subtitles filter, safe margins and large font. 5. Mix voice with subtle licensed music and effects. 6. Export H.264 + AAC MP4, 1080×1920, 30 fps. 7. `ffprobe -show_streams output.mp4` to verify both audio and video streams; play and listen for naturalness; inspect frames to confirm captions always visible during speech. No MP4 was rendered in this package.
